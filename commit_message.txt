Link audio lifecycle tests to hvm_core and generate the tone (#186)

The host test was compiling Mixer.cpp beside hvm_core, so it could drift
from the mixer the emulator runs. It now links hvm_core. The guest WAV is
produced by the Test Lab generator when the app is packaged, under the name
tone.wav the guest looks up. The Linux report runs that host binary and the
guest app, so a miss fails the job.

The trusted CI report publishers were failing after a cancelled Linux or
macOS job because that job uploads no artifact. They publish when the job
finished with success or failure, and they stay idle when the job was
cancelled or skipped. A real test failure still publishes its report.

Continues PR #186.
